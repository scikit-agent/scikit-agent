Models
--------

Models written as scikit-agent blocks: decision problems, games, and the
diagrams that state their structure. A block says who decides what, knowing
what, and paid for what, which is a general enough description that the models
here are drawn from economics, from game theory and from the AI-safety
literature alike -- and the list is open.

Some of these models are solved for a policy, with reinforcement learning or
other computational methods. Others are read rather than solved: what a decision
must account for, and what an agent has an incentive to observe or to move, are
properties of a model's structure, and are answerable before any policy is
computed.
